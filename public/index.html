Which federal contracts are worth reading?

The last seven days of federal IT opportunities, read and judged. Nobody wants to read sixty procurement notices to find the few that matter.

Federal prime contracts only. SAM.gov carries no state, local or education procurement, and federal bidding needs SAM registration. A small agency will find real work here, but not all of it is realistically winnable — which is exactly what the score is for.
